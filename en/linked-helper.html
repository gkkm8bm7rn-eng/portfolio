Linked Helper

A product landing-page concept based on a test assignment: research, positioning, structure, and responsive implementation. Not a client launch.

Research and message

The work began with keyword research, competitor analysis, and public reviews. The core message combines personalization with control over messages.

Page architecture

Deliverables included value mapping, a message house, and a 16-block page sequence. The copy explains product mechanics without unsupported conversion metrics.

Visual principle

The hero concept shows the path from prospect context to a draft and review. It is a schematic interface, rather than a screenshot of a finished product.

Outcome

A lightweight HTML/CSS/JavaScript implementation was prepared with mobile navigation and accessible structure. It remains an additional project outside the studio’s selected furniture and catalog work.
