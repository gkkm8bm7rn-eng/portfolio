A website built around the collection

The verified structure contains 22 content pages: home, collection, model pages, production information, and a journal. The collection supports model search, and photographs can be opened at a larger size.

Restrained typography and large images keep the furniture in focus. A dedicated menu supports mobile navigation. The site demonstrates structure and interface; it has no connected inquiry system, cart, or payment flow.

02 / GPT pricing assistant

Compare upholstery options without waiting for a quote

The custom GPT calculates modular sofas from a supplied price list: selected modules, fabric categories, extra elements and the checked total, with a clear breakdown.

Seeing the difference between upholstery options helps buyers decide whether they can afford a higher-tier finish. That creates an opportunity for a larger order value, although a measured sales uplift is not claimed.

Start with a real product

A showroom photograph and marked-up details establish the form, proportions, and areas to change. Visualization brings them together into a view that can be discussed.

Product decisions and material selection are the author’s own work. AI supports the visualization process.

More about form and materials

Product and terms in one document

The Palermo commercial proposal brings together the product image, dimensions, configuration, material, and pricing terms. The customer can review the information in one place.

This is an existing single-page portfolio document. It demonstrates commercial layout, rather than a completed multi-page PDF catalog. Prices in the image belong to the source document.

05 / Documents

One calculation — consistent paperwork

The pricing configuration can feed a draft specification and commercial offer, reducing manual duplication of the modules, materials and price breakdown.

Document drafting and checking are supported by GPT; no integration with a CRM or accounting system is claimed.
