Contribution
Product decisions, texture and color combinations, and visualization review.

More than upholstery color

Selecting real fabrics involves texture scale, surface combinations, and budget. A material needs to work with the form, beyond looking good on a single sample.

Material cost and sample availability are checked for each commission.

An image to review the decision

Textured orange upholstery and a dark background show the character of the proposed direction. Visualization presents an idea before production; the fabric still needs to be reviewed in person.

AI helps prepare the image. Form, fabric, and combination decisions remain the author’s product design work. This image does not establish a finished manufacturing result.

A distinct discipline within the project

Material selection defines the object itself. The catalog and website help communicate that decision to a customer. In OTUTTO, these stages are presented as parts of one project.
