Form begins
with material.

An OTUTTO sofa study: working with proportions, upholstery details, texture, and color before preparing the final visual.

Brief
Define the product’s character through form and upholstery.
Basis
A real product and material selection.
Contribution
Product decisions, texture and color combinations, and visualization review.

A real product comes first

The source was a real sofa photographed in neutral upholstery. Its construction, proportions and seam lines were retained as reference points while exploring a different material and visual direction.

The goal was not merely to recolor the photo, but to make the proposed upholstery credible on the existing form.

Material follows the form

The marked-up areas connect the upholstery decision with the sofa’s construction. Texture, seams and contrast must look coherent together — and work within the material budget.

The product decisions, material selection and review are the designer’s work; AI was used to support the visualization.

A distinct discipline within the project

Material selection defines the object itself. The catalog and website help communicate that decision to a customer. In OTUTTO, these stages are presented as parts of one project.
